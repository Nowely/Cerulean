# Design decisions the client follows

One entry per decision: the date, the decision in one sentence, where it comes from, and where it lives in
`Client/`. Read the entries for an area before changing how it behaves. When a PR changes or adds a decision,
it adds or edits the entry here in the same PR.

Sources:

- **spec**: `spec/spec.md` in the mockups project. The lines are marked with the version that set them, such as **v21**.
- **delta**: a handoff file such as `handoff/v21-delta.md`. Its item numbers and the review ids (F…, V…, C…, O…) are used below.
- **QA**: `design/app-qa/report.md`, which lists bugs as B… and fresh-eyes findings as U….
- **Josh**: Josh's message in the project, with its date and time.

## Product and stack

- **2026-10-19 · The app is a browser-only SPA, offline first, with no backend.** Data lives in IndexedDB on the device. Source: Josh, 19 Oct. Code: `nuxt.config.ts` (`ssr: false`), `app/db/`.
- **2026-10-19 · Авто is a model call from the browser with the user's own key, or simple rules without one.** Source: Josh, 19 Oct. Code: `app/domain/model.ts`, `app/domain/rules.ts`.
- **2026-10-20 · Keep it simple.** No IndexedDB migrations or data protection, no sync, and CI runs only lint, types and tests (no screenshot checks). Source: Josh, 20 Oct 08:30. Code: `.github/workflows/client.yml`.
- **2026-10-20 · Desktop and phone share one logic.** They differ only in containers: sheet against popover, swipe against hover. Source: Josh, 20 Oct 19:09. Code: `ActionMenu.vue` and `AdaptiveOverlay.vue` (one component, two containers).
- **2026-10-21 · The installed app starts offline.** The SPA shell is prerendered to `index.html` and precached. Source: QA B2. Code: `nuxt.config.ts` (`nitro.prerender`, `pwa.workbox`).

## Look

- **2026-10-20 · The approved theme pair.** Light is «Строгий, плоский» (accent #1F5F8B, background #FCFCFB); dark is blue-graphite (background #161F26, accent #78B2DF). There are no gradients. Colours live only in `tokens.css`, and components use no colours, `:deep` or `!important`. Source: Josh, 20 Oct 11:35; spec «Visual language». Code: `app/assets/css/tokens.css`, then `main.css`, then `app.config.ts`.
- **2026-10-20 · Quiet first.** Colour is used only for meaning (accent, warn, ok), surfaces are separated by fill rather than borders, and each screen has one prominent thing. Source: spec v17. Code: throughout. Check new components against it.
- **2026-10-21 · Overdue reads the same everywhere.** It is the word «было …» in warn, and a warn dot in Календарь. Source: QA B17; delta v21 item 17. Code: `RecordRow.vue`, `CalendarLayout.vue`, `DueButton.vue`.

## Writing loop

- **2026-10-20 · One composer everywhere.** It is docked at the bottom, with the destination chip on the left. Source: port delta. Code: `AppComposer.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · Day one writes without pages.** When a template fits, Авто makes that page in «Мои страницы» and the first row says «новая страница из шаблона». The guess line says so before the send («→ Покупки»). «Отменить» removes the record and the page. When nothing fits, the Уточнение offers the starters not made yet. Source: delta v21 item 1 (F1, idea 1); acceptance of PR #15, item 6. Code: `stores/journal.ts`, `rules.ts` (`templateFor`), `PlaceChips.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · The aim from «Отменить» is one-shot.** The text comes back aimed at the same page («→ Продукты, только эта запись»), and Авто returns after that send or when the text is cleared. Source: delta v21 item 2 (F2); QA B8. Code: `AppComposer.vue` (`once`).
- **2026-10-21 · One way to say «записано».** A send that lands on another page docks the receipt, Уточнение or found card above the composer, on every screen. Records never announce themselves with a toast, and a toast and a docked card never show together. Source: delta v21 item 3 (F6); spec «Toast». Code: `ComposerDock.vue`, `useNotify.ts` (`ui.toastAt`).
- **2026-10-21 · The receipt.** Its header reads «N записей → Page» with «Не туда» as a quiet button. The footer holds «Отменить» while its 15 s last, and «в Журнале ›» when docked on Главная, which stays after «Отменить» goes. Undo names what it takes back («Отменить «Яблоки»», «Отменить последние 2»). The receipt stays docked until another replaces it, also across a visit to another screen: what each screen's dock shows lives in the ui store. Source: delta v21 items 4 and 5 (F5, C6, C8); QA B9; acceptance of PR #15, items 1 and 2. Code: `ReceiptCard.vue`, `ComposerDock.vue`, `stores/ui.ts` (`docks`).
- **2026-10-21 · A recognised date is an accent button that opens the «Срок» sheet.** The sheet offers Сегодня, Завтра, В субботу, Без срока and time chips. There is no editing by words and no «Другое время…». Source: delta v21 items 7 and 26 (idea 2, reduced; Josh, 20 Oct 19:09). Code: `DueButton.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · Mic.** It listens with a wave and «Слушаю…»; «Остановить» leaves the words as a draft. Offline the mic is faint, and a tap says «Диктовка без сети не работает». Source: delta v21 item 8 (V7). Code: `AppComposer.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · Входящие chips say what they do** («→ Задачи», «Ещё…»), and a pick toasts with «Отменить». Guesses are recomputed from the current pages. A line a starter fits, with no matching page, offers the starter rather than an unrelated recent page. Source: delta v21 item 9 (O1, F3); QA B13; QA U5. Code: `RecordRow.vue`, `stores/journal.ts` (`guessesFor`), `rules.ts` (`classify`).
- **2026-10-21 · A send splits by commas, semicolons and new lines on every page and in Авто** (on Покупки «и» splits too and «купить» is dropped). In Авто the parts go to their own pages under one receipt only when their clues point to two or more places; a part without a clue joins its neighbour, so «молоко, вафли» stays together. The guess line counts before the send («Покупки · 2 записи», «Покупки 2, Задачи 1»). Source: delta v22 item 7 (U3, G15). Code: `domain/parse.ts` (`splitParts`), `domain/auto.ts` (`splitRoute`, `preview`), `stores/journal.ts` (`placeParts`).
- **2026-10-21 · Receipt rows have no checkboxes.** A row shows a dot, or the page glyph when the rows went to several pages; each row has its own «→» (`chooseOne`). Source: delta v22 items 8 and 33 (G9, N4). Code: `ReceiptCard.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · Questions are written down.** Only «найти/найди/где/поиск/искать…» give the found card; anything else is filed like any send with its «?», and up to three similar records show under the receipt as «Похожее в записях». Text is kept as typed; a routing prefix Авто understood («идея:») is dropped. Source: delta v22 items 10 and 22 (U10, U11, B33). Code: `domain/parse.ts` (`isSearch`, `cleanRecordText`, `dropPrefix`), `stores/journal.ts` (`decide`).
- **2026-10-21 · When the AI fails, the record is still written.** No answer in 10 s: the record waits in Входящие with «ИИ не ответил…» · «Повторить»; «Нет сети» only when the browser is offline. A refused key: filed by the simple rules with a quiet line and «Проверить ключ». The pending card keeps the sent words with a small spinner. Source: delta v22 items 9, 34 and 35 (G2, B6, B7, N6, N7). Code: `domain/model.ts` (`TIMEOUT`), `stores/journal.ts` (`wait`, `retry`, `keyRefused`), `PendingCard.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · A refused save gives the text back.** When the browser refuses the write (no space), the card «Не сохранилось…» shows with «Повторить» and «Освободить место ›», the text returns to the field, and nothing claims it was saved. Source: delta v22 item 9 (B3). Code: `stores/data.ts` (`writeFailed`), `ComposerDock.vue`, `AppComposer.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · The composer chip is accent-ink and shows the destination's glyph first** (Авто's fork on Главная). Chips on a page's background sit on `trk`. Source: delta v22 item 24; acceptance of PR #15, items 7 and 8. Code: `DestChip.vue`, `ChipButton.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · One notice slot per screen (v22 item 11).** A notice sits in the flow right above the composer or the docked card, never over the list; the topmost open sheet shows it at its foot; the newest replaces the old one. With «Отменить» it lasts 8 s, otherwise 5 s; hover, focus and a finger pause it; long text wraps to two lines and the action is never cut. On desktop it sits at the column's right with a drain line; over a dialog it rises above the scrim. It still replaces a docked card (v21), except «ИИ снова не ответил», which keeps it. It outlives navigation until it ends. Source: delta v22 item 11 (U9, V4, B11, B20). Code: `useNotify.ts`, `shell/NoticeSlot.vue` (Nuxt UI's toaster is off).
- **2026-10-21 · The docked Уточнение is short (v22 item 12):** question, one row of page chips that scrolls sideways and fades at the edge, footnote, about 150 px; in Журнал the chips wrap. Source: delta v22 item 12 (D5). Code: `QuestionCard.vue`, `PlaceChips.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · Duplicates are written as typed (v22 item 25).** A record matching an open top-level record on the same page (not from the same send) reads «уже есть в списке, 2 л» with «Объединить» while the receipt is fresh or docked; merging removes the new record, adds same-unit quantities and changes the row in place to «объединено с записью в списке», with no notice. A new page named like an existing one says «Страница «X» уже есть в секции Y.» · «Открыть» (which drops the new page while it is empty). Source: delta v22 item 25 (G3). Code: `domain/merge.ts`, `ReceiptCard.vue`, `PageView.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · «Запомнить» (v22 item 26)** is offered only after a page is chosen by hand (Уточнение, «Не туда», «Ещё…», a Входящие choice), not after a row's «→» or «Оставить там». It appends «Например: «…»» to that page's description and then reads «Запомнено в описании «X».» · «Отменить» on the same line. A hand choice clears the slow-model or key note on that receipt. Source: delta v22 item 26 (U5). Code: `journal.ts` (`learn`), `ReceiptCard.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · Уточнение never offers «Новая страница «X»» when X exists (v22 item 39):** it offers the existing page. Source: delta v22 item 39 (N23). Code: `rules.ts` (`placeOptions`).
- **2026-10-21 · Plain words for the AI (v22 item 35):** «ИИ не ответил, запись ждёт во Входящих.» · «Повторить»; «Разложено по простым правилам: ключ не принят.» · «Проверить ключ»; «модель» only in Настройки. «Похожее в записях» rows open the record in the right panel on desktop and the preview sheet on a phone. Source: delta v22 items 22 and 35 (N7). Code: `ReceiptCard.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · The receipt head says «перемещено» quietly before «Не туда».** On desktop the head is a link to the page in the right panel, with no chevron; a folded one-line receipt keeps its «›». Source: mockup Home, HomeLearn. Code: `ReceiptCard.vue`.

## Pages, records, views

- **2026-10-21 · Titles are edited in place.** They wrap and are never cut. Enter or leaving the field saves, Esc reverts, and «Переименовать» in «⋯» focuses the title. Source: delta v21 items 11 and 27 (V3); QA B5. Code: `TitleField.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · Views.** There is no «+» after the tabs: «Новый вид» lives in «Настроить вид», and Сегодня has no view bar. A «Выбор» property cannot be saved without options. Source: delta v21 item 14 (O6, O3, C3); QA B12. Code: `PageView.vue`, `ViewSettings.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · Доска.** On a phone it shows one column at a time, with a wrapping strip of columns. Every card has «В «next» →»; on desktop it shows on hover. Its «⋯» starts with «Открыть», then the columns. A move toasts with «Отменить», and the open column stays where it is. Source: mockups Board/MobileBoard; QA B13, B26, G7. Code: `BoardLayout.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · A long list shows 100 rows and «Показать ещё».** Menu sheets mount only when first opened. With 600 records, opening a page went from 9.7 s to 0.7 s. A row written while the page is open always renders, in its group, past the cut, and scrolls into view: a send never lands out of sight. Source: QA B4; acceptance of PR #15, item 3. Code: `ListLayout.vue`, `RecordRow.vue` (`reveal`), `ActionMenu.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · A row swipe uses `touch-action: pan-y`,** so the browser keeps vertical scroll and leaves the sideways drag to the row. The actions grow from the right edge over the row's values; the mark and the name stay in place. Source: QA B1; acceptance of PR #15, item 5. Code: `RecordRow.vue`, `useSwipe.ts`.
- **2026-10-21 · Density (v22).** Record rows 44 px with 16 px text (15 on desktop), page title 24, page header 44, Главная header 52 and its rows 48, group captions 12 px above. Rows show properties, never when they were made; the subitem count comes before the note icon; a fresh record has only the fading `fresh` tint. Source: delta v22 points 1 and 5 (Josh, 20 Oct 19:29). Code: `RecordRow.vue`, `ListLayout.vue`, `PageView.vue`, `HomeHeader.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · A tick never makes a row vanish.** The row stays struck through while the page is open and moves to «Куплено»/«Готово» when the page is opened again. Source: delta v22 item 6 (U2). Code: `domain/views.ts` (`applyView`, `since`), `Rec.tickedAt`.
- **2026-10-21 · The view menu.** A tap on the current tab (long press on any tab, right-click on desktop) opens it: name and type, «Настроить вид», «Новый вид», «Удалить вид» with «Отменить». The last view cannot be deleted. A new view is named after its type and follows it until renamed. Source: delta v22 points 2, 15 (U14, D3). Code: `ViewTabs.vue`, `domain/templates.ts` (`nameForLayout`).
- **2026-10-21 · Group captions fold** («Хлеб и выпечка · 3 ›»), stay sticky and are remembered per page and view. Source: delta v22 item 13 (G5). Code: `ListLayout.vue`, `data.toggleFolded`.
- **2026-10-21 · A page with no records has no view bar.** Views come from «⋯ → Настроить вид». Source: mockup MobileBlank; acceptance of PR #15, item 12. Code: `PageView.vue` (`bar`).
- **2026-10-21 · Drag (v22).** On a phone a row is lifted by a long press; on desktop only by the ⋮⋮ handle that shows on hover, or by Alt+↑/↓, which also crosses into the next group. A drop into another group sets that group's value («Яйца» into «Овощи и фрукты» changes Отдел). A drop under a sort that is not «вручную» switches the view to «вручную» and says so with «Отменить». Code: `RecordRow.vue`, `ListLayout.vue`, `useDragSort.ts`, `data.placeRecord`.
- **2026-10-21 · Календарь (v22).** The switch between month and week reads in words; under a selected day only the time shows. A record written while a day is selected lands on that day, an undated one included, and the calendar stays open on it. Source: delta v22 items 16, 38. Code: `CalendarLayout.vue`, `useCalendarDay.ts`.
- **2026-10-21 · The shop loop (v22).** A bought row's tick reads «Купить снова». «Очистить купленное» shows only on a view of done records and has «Отменить». Move and delete notices stay 8 s. Source: delta v22 items 40, 11. Code: `RecordMark.vue`, `ListLayout.vue`, `data.clearDone`.
- **2026-10-21 · An emptied board column stays selected,** and «Без статуса» stays in the strip while it is the selected column. Source: delta v22 item 17. Code: `BoardLayout.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · Edges that read in dark (v22).** An off switch has a `trk` track, a `line2` edge and a ringed knob; a lifted row has a `ring` edge. Source: delta v22 item 41. Code: `app.config.ts`, `RecordRow.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · A segmented control never cuts a label.** When a label measures as cut, the control becomes one row that opens a list sheet. Source: delta v22 item 14 (G8). Code: `SegmentedControl.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · Tabs that do not fit fade at the edge,** and the first tab is always whole. Source: delta v22 item 14. Code: `ViewTabs.vue`.
- **2026-10-21 · The Задачи demo page has Список, Календарь and Готово views,** so the calendar and the done view can be tried on day one. Code: `domain/seed.ts`.

Code: `PropSheets.vue`, `PropEditor.vue`, `RecordView.vue`, `domain/props.ts`, `useDragSort.ts`.
